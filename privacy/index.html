1. Scope

Bee Trainer is a spelling and vocabulary app for iPhone and iPad. The first release is free and unlimited. It does not require an account and does not provide cloud synchronization.

2. Voice, pronunciation and permissions

Voice spelling uses Apple’s on-device US English speech recognition. The app requires on-device recognition support and does not fall back to uploading voice audio or transcripts to a recognition server. Microphone and speech-recognition permissions are requested for voice practice. Audio recordings are not saved by the app. Recognized text and letters may appear in local practice records or diagnostics.

Word and meaning pronunciation uses installed system voices. Recognition and voice assets depend on device support and availability. You can use keyboard practice if recognition is unsupported or permissions are denied. Manage permissions in iOS Settings.

3. Local learning data and images

Wordbooks, words, custom meanings, examples, notes, practice attempts and review-plan records are stored in the app’s local storage. Saved memory images are stored in app-owned files. Review records include local profile/device identifiers for reconstructing learning history; these are not login accounts and are not sent to a sync service. The app bundles an offline English dictionary. Your custom meanings take priority over automatic definitions.

4. Camera, selected photos and text recognition

Printed-list import uses the system document scanner or photos you select. Text recognition runs locally using Apple Vision. You review and edit the recognized words before saving them. The app does not upload scanned pages or selected photos to an OCR service.

5. Network requests and third parties

The app retrieves public version configuration from this website to check for updates. These requests expose normal network metadata, such as your IP address, to the hosting provider, Tencent EdgeOne. They do not include your wordbooks, learning history or voice audio. A cached required-update policy can continue to require an update while offline.

The first-release configuration has no remote meaning-generation or backend endpoint enabled. There are no advertising, third-party analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs in this build. This static website has no tracking scripts, forms or external runtime assets.

6. Exports, backups and support requests

You can export wordbooks as text-only JSON, or export a Learning backup containing wordbooks, saved images, spelling history and review records. Both formats exclude reusable content and associations. Exports leave the app only through the system file picker or share action you choose. A storage or sharing provider you select may process that file under its own terms. App data may also be included in device/system backups, depending on your settings.

Diagnostics remain local unless you explicitly export and share them. They may include recognized text, spellings, timing and device/OS information. If you email support, the information you send is processed to respond to your request; do not include sensitive information. [CONFIRM SUPPORT: mailbox provider, access and retention.]

7. Retention and deletion

Learning data remains in the app until you remove it. Books and words can be managed in Books; deleting a word may retain compact historical practice records. Settings provides separate reusable-cache and saved-image cleanup controls. Deleting the app using iOS Delete App removes its local app data; Offload App keeps data. Device backups, exported files and copies shared with others must be managed separately. Contact [CONFIRM SUPPORT EMAIL] about information you sent to support and applicable access, correction or deletion requests.

8. Children and family use

Parents and guardians should supervise permissions, exported files and support messages when children use the app. The first release has no accounts, advertising, chat or social sharing feed. Do not include a child’s personal information in vocabulary notes or support requests. 9. Security and processing locations

Local data is kept in the app’s iOS sandbox. Keep your device secured and protect exported files, which contain readable learning data. Network version checks use HTTPS. No service can promise absolute security. Hosting, support-email and user-selected backup providers may process information outside your country; their specific settings and applicable safeguards require owner confirmation.
