Common questions

Why can’t the app hear me?

Check microphone permission in device settings, confirm that the microphone is not blocked, and try again in a quiet location. On iOS, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. Labels may vary by OS version.

Why isn’t speech recognition working?

Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Speech Recognition on iOS. Confirm your device language and network connection if the released app uses a network recognition service. Supported languages, offline availability, and provider requirements are pending confirmation.

How do I delete vocabulary or restore progress?

Deletion, backup, and recovery behavior must be confirmed for the released app. Do not uninstall as a troubleshooting step until you know whether your local data is backed up.

How do I report a bug?

Contact the support email once published. Include your device model, OS version, Bee Trainer version, steps to reproduce, and the result you expected. Remove personal information from screenshots. Do not send passwords, sensitive vocabulary, or audio recordings.

How do I get help with a purchase?

Paid-feature availability has not been confirmed. If monetization is introduced, contact support for app issues. For App Store billing or refund requests, follow Apple’s purchase support process and applicable terms. Android purchase guidance will be added when available.
