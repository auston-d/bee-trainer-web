Getting started

  1. Open Books, create a wordbook and add words. You can also import a printed list using the camera or selected photos; check the recognized words before saving.
  2. Open Study, choose Memory or Competition, select your books and start. Use the on-screen keyboard or voice spelling where available.
  3. Tap the bee beside a word to save it for review. Today’s review on Study offers small rounds when words are ready.
  4. Open Settings using the control at the upper right of Study or Books for backups, dictionary credits, privacy, support and diagnostics.

Common questions

Can I use Bee Trainer offline?

Wordbooks, keyboard practice, local review scheduling and the bundled English dictionary work offline. Voice spelling also needs supported on-device English recognition and its required assets. Preparing these assets may require a connection through iOS. If recognition is unavailable, use the keyboard. The app periodically checks a public version configuration when connected. A previously downloaded required update remains required offline; an initial failed check without a valid cached policy does not block learning.

Why can’t the app hear me?

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Bee Trainer in iOS Settings. Check that the microphone is not obstructed. Voice spelling requires supported on-device US English recognition; the app does not fall back to uploading audio for recognition. Use keyboard input if permissions are denied or recognition is unavailable.

Why is pronunciation missing or different?

Install an English voice in Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak (Spoken Content on older iOS) → Voices → English. Bee Trainer prefers the best installed US English voice. Available voices depend on your device and installed assets.

How do I back up and restore my learning?

In Settings, choose Export learning backup to include wordbooks, saved images, spelling history and the review plan. Import learning backup adds missing records while preserving existing content; reusable content and associations are excluded. Export wordbooks creates a smaller text-only file containing books, word order, custom meanings, context and notes; it excludes history and images. Keep exported files somewhere you can access later and import the corresponding backup type. These are manual backups, not automatic cloud sync.

Export before removing an older development installation: changing the app’s bundle identifier creates a separate data directory and does not transfer its contents automatically.

How do I delete words, images or all app data?

Manage words and wordbooks in Books. Removing a word does not erase every historical practice snapshot. Settings → Delete saved memory images removes saved images; Clear reusable content cache keeps private learning data. To remove the app’s local data, use Delete App in iOS Settings rather than Offload App. Export first if you want to retain your learning. Delete exported files and manage device or cloud backups separately; deleting the app cannot remove copies you previously shared.

Why did an imported word look wrong?

Printed-list recognition runs on the device and can misread valid-looking words. Crop the word-list region, inspect the source preview, and edit or deselect words before import. Automatic dictionary definitions are general WordNet definitions; you can enter a more suitable meaning yourself.

Are there purchases or subscriptions?

The first release is free and unlimited, with no purchase or subscription required. Future paid features, if introduced, will have their own pricing and purchase details.

How do I report a problem?

Email the support address above. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce and what you expected. Do not send passwords, audio recordings or sensitive vocabulary. Diagnostics can be exported from Settings → Diagnostics when needed; inspect the file before sharing because it can contain recognized text, target spellings and practice details.
